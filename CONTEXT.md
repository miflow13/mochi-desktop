# Mochi Desktop Companion

Mochi is a desktop companion whose behavior, presentation, and relationship progression remain intentionally separate. This glossary records the relationship terms used across the app.

## Language

**Bond**:
Persistent, non-decaying relationship progress represented by a level and XP.
_Avoid_: Affection score, friendship meter

**Bond Phase**:
The familiarity band derived from Bond level that determines the tone and eligible pool of relationship dialogue. It is New, Familiar, Comfortable, Close, or Deep Bond.
_Avoid_: Mood, emotion, personality state

**Mood**:
Mochi's current interpreted emotional or activity state, independent of Bond and Bond Phase.
_Avoid_: Bond phase, relationship level

**Relationship Dialogue**:
Short direct-interaction dialogue whose available lines are determined by the current Bond Phase and exact bond level.
_Avoid_: Ambient dialogue, contextual reaction
