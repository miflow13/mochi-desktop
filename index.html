01 / not a widget

He’s meant to feel alive, not productive.

No streaks. No guilt. No “you missed yesterday.” Mochi is just a small character sharing your desktop with you.

ambient://personality● ● ●

He notices the broad stuff.

Typing, coding, music, terminals, idle time, power changes—and then decides whether to react.

bond://local● ● ●

He remembers time together.

Bond grows through normal interaction, without decay or punishment for going away.

state://nap● ● ●

Sometimes he just falls asleep.

Honestly? Same.

04 / AmbiSense

Aware of your desktop.
Not reading it.

Mochi’s awareness is local and rule-based. It works from coarse signals, not your words, document names, window titles, or screen contents.
